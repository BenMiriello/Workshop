# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Supersedes / superseded by: (optional, link the other record)

## Context

What forces the decision. The problem, the constraints, the alternatives on the
table, and any facts (file paths, prior verdicts, plan docs) that ground it. State
what is true today, not aspirations.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly and in the present tense. One decision per record. Name
the concrete mechanism (module, path, convention) it lands on.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. What is now settled and should not be
re-litigated. Any follow-on work, escape hatches, or explicitly-deferred pieces.
